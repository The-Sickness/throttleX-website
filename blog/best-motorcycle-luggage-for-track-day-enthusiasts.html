Best Motorcycle Luggage for Track Day Enthusiasts

The roar of the engine, the smell of burning rubber, and the thrill of leaning into a corner at full speed make track days the ultimate playground for sportbike riders. But getting to the track with all the essential gear can be a puzzle. Riders need to transport helmets, tool kits, tire warmers, canopy chairs, and a change of clothes without compromising the sleek, aerodynamic profile of their machines. Strapping down a duffel bag with bungee cords is not only unsafe but can ruin the paintwork and shift at high speeds.

For riders who demand performance on both asphalt and the highway, selecting the right storage is key. Viking Bags, an industry-leading manufacturer of motorcycle luggage and aftermarket parts, builds a range of bags designed to meet the unique needs of track day warriors. Here is a complete guide to the best motorcycle luggage options for track day enthusiasts and how to choose the perfect setup for your next visit to the circuit.

THE UNIQUE STORAGE NEEDS OF A TRACK DAY RIDER

Unlike long-distance touring, track days require a specific type of cargo. The luggage needs to be compact enough to avoid drag but spacious enough to carry the necessities. A track day rig typically includes a rear stand, an EZ-up canopy, fuel cans, and a toolbox. These items are usually left in the pit with a friend or in a support vehicle. However, the rider is still responsible for safely transporting their personal riding gear to the track.

What does a track day rider usually carry on the bike:

  • Helmet: a full-face helmet is bulky and requires a bag for protection
  • Back protector and leathers: soft bags are ideal to prevent creasing hard armor
  • Hydration and nutrition: for hot days on track, water and energy bars are a must
  • Tire pressure gauges and warmers: advanced group riders need space for warmers

The ideal luggage for this scenario must be secure and quick to mount. A flapping strap or a poorly secured bag can be a major distraction at 150 mph down the front straight. The choice of luggage is just as important as the choice of tires.

SADDLEBAGS: BALANCING STABILITY AND STORAGE

When people think of motorcycle luggage, saddlebags are usually the first thing that comes to mind. However, for sportbikes, traditional wide leather bags are a no-go. They disrupt airflow and can get singed by high-mounted exhausts. Modern engineering has produced streamlined options that work with a sportbike's geometry rather than against it.

For track day enthusiasts who ride street bikes to the circuit, Viking Bags has developed the Viking Momentum Extra Large Expandable Street Sportsbike Saddlebags, specifically designed with sportbike geometry in mind.

Why these saddlebags work for track days:

  • Expandable capacity: on the way to the track the bags expand to fit a back protector and riding suit. After the session they compress to maintain a slim profile for the ride home
  • Cordura construction: built to withstand regular use and resist wear over time
  • Secure mounting: mounting straps with Velcro attachment keep them stable during the ride, reliable for highway stretches to the circuit

TAIL BAGS: THE TRACK DAY SWEET SPOT

If there is one type of luggage that perfectly suits the track day crowd, it is the tail bag. Mounting the gear over the passenger seat keeps the weight centralized and has the least impact on handling. Unlike saddlebags which sit on the sides, a tail bag keeps the bike's profile narrow, allowing for lean angles without scraping the bags on the asphalt.

The Viking Momentum Extra Large Street Sportbike Tail Bag is a prime example of form meeting function. With 2,520 cubic inches of storage in its main compartment, it offers more than enough room for stashing a helmet and riding gear when off the bike.

Key features:

  • Generous main compartment: 2,520 cubic inches comfortably fits a full-face helmet and additional riding essentials
  • Sportbike-tailored design: compact purpose-built profile compatible with the tight lines of modern sportbikes
  • Roping hook mounting system: heavy-duty roping hooks with a drawstring keep the bag anchored to the passenger seat, preventing shifting during hard acceleration or braking

For riders who prefer a minimalist setup, the Viking Momentum Small Street Sportbike Tail Bag features a dedicated interior zipper pocket for keys, wallets, phones, and small tools. It maintains the bike's sleek look while offering just enough utility for riders meeting friends at the track who already have a canopy and tools waiting in the pit.

BACKPACKS: THE RIDER-LEVEL CONVENIENCE

Sometimes the simplest solution is the best. A high-quality motorcycle backpack distributes the weight across the rider's body so the bike handles exactly as it does during a normal ride. There is no added weight over the rear wheel to cause headshake or instability.

However, wearing a backpack on the track comes with a caveat: fatigue. A standard backpack will cut into the shoulders and cause sweating. This is where specialized gear comes in.

Viking Bags offers tactical backpacks built for high-performance riding. The Viking 30-Liter Tactical Large Motorcycle Backpack is a standout choice for track-goers:

  • Padded shoulder straps: provide comfort on long highway stretches to the circuit, reducing fatigue before the riding even begins
  • Chest and waist straps: adjustable sternum clips and waist straps prevent the backpack from shifting at speed and keep the load stable and close to the body
  • Modular storage with water bottle holders: multiple pockets allow riders to organize tools separately from snacks. Two 25 oz side water bottle holders make staying hydrated on hot track days easy and accessible

For maximum cargo capacity, the Viking Tactical Extra Large Motorcycle Backpack offers even more room with an expanded main compartment and dual 25 oz water bottle holders, a practical choice for riders heading to a full-day track event.

TANK BAGS: ESSENTIAL TOOLS AT YOUR FINGERTIPS

Tank bags are often overlooked by track day riders, but they serve a vital purpose. When in the pit, a tank bag acts as a mobile command center for phones, cameras, earplugs, and sunglasses, all within easy reach without dismounting.

The Viking Magnetic Street Sportbike Tank Bag attaches securely to the steel tank of most sportbikes via a strong magnetic grip system with a rubberized anti-scratch underside to protect the tank's finish. Its top-mounted touchscreen-compatible smartphone and GPS viewing window keeps navigation and timing apps visible while riding.

Considerations for tank bag use:

  • Paint protection: ensure both the tank surface and the underside of the bag are clean before mounting, as trapped debris can cause scratching
  • Accessibility: fully accessible while sitting on the bike, making it easy to grab sunglasses or earplugs between sessions without dismounting
  • Smartphone compatibility: the touchscreen-compatible top window lets riders use navigation or timing apps without removing the phone from the bag

HOW TO CHOOSE THE RIGHT SETUP

Not every track day is the same. Sometimes a rider is going solo; sometimes they are meeting a group with a support truck. Here is how to decide which Viking bag to take:

  • The solo rider with no support vehicle: needs to carry everything. The best combination is the Momentum Expandable Saddlebags for bulk storage paired with the Momentum Extra Large Tail Bag for the helmet. This combo maximizes space without needing a luggage rack
  • The minimalist meeting friends: if there is a canopy and tools already at the track, the rider just needs personal gear. A Viking Tactical Backpack carries the essentials and keeps the bike completely free of mounts
  • The photographer and rider: for those who like to document their day, a tank bag keeps the phone or camera safe from vibration and easily accessible between sessions. Pair with a small tail bag for tools and personal items

DURABILITY MEETS DESIGN

One of the biggest fears for any sportbike owner is that luggage will ruin the look of their machine. Viking Bags addresses this by using high-quality Cordura material that matches the aggressive lines of modern street bikes, ensuring the bags are both weatherproof and visually appropriate for sportbike aesthetics.

Riders have reported that Viking products hold up exceptionally well over time, with rugged zippers and securement straps built to last through regular use.

FINAL VERDICT

Track days are about pushing limits, but getting to the track should not be a hassle. The right luggage system enhances the entire experience, allowing riders to focus on their lap times rather than worrying about whether their gear will fly off at speed. Whether opting for the expanded storage of the Momentum Saddlebags, the centralized weight of the Extra Large Tail Bag, or the hands-free convenience of a Tactical Backpack, the key is choosing gear that matches the intensity of the ride.

Viking Bags provides a comprehensive range of solutions built specifically for street and sportbike riders. From the expandable Momentum series to the versatile tactical backpacks and the magnetic tank bag, there is an option for every track day enthusiast. Gear up, pack smart, and enjoy the ride to the track, because the journey there should be just as rewarding as the laps around it.
