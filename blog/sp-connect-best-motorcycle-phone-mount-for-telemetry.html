SP Connect: The Best Motorcycle Phone Mount for Telemetry Riders

By ThrottleX Team Published August 15, 2026 Read time 9 min

Most riders think about their phone mount for about five minutes before buying the cheapest thing on Amazon. That is a mistake when you are using the phone for telemetry. The mount is not just a holder. It is the physical interface between the bike and every sensor reading the app produces. A bad mount degrades your data. A great mount makes the data reliable session after session without any drift, noise, or repositioning.

The ThrottleX development bike runs the SP Connect Moto Stem Mount with the Anti Vibration Module. It has been on the same bike through canyon runs, track days, and thousands of miles without moving a millimeter from the position it was set to on day one. This article explains why that matters and why SP Connect is the mount system we recommend without hesitation to any serious telemetry rider.

WHY THE MOUNT MATTERS MORE THAN MOST RIDERS THINK

Your phone measures lean angle and G-force using its built-in IMU (the inertial measurement unit), a combination of gyroscopes and accelerometers that detect orientation and movement. These sensors are sensitive by design. They are engineered to detect the subtle movements of a phone in a human hand. When you strap that phone to a machine producing continuous vibration from an engine, road surface, and wind, the IMU picks up all of it.

ThrottleX uses a Kalman filter to process the sensor stream and remove high-frequency vibration noise from the lean angle and G-force calculations. The filter works significantly better when vibration is minimized at the source. A rigid mount that isolates the phone from handlebar vibration gives the filter cleaner input data and produces more accurate readings throughout the session.

Beyond sensor accuracy there is the physical reliability question. A mount that allows even subtle movement during hard cornering introduces inconsistency into every data point collected after that movement. Your lean angle calibration was set with the phone at one angle. If the mount shifts by two degrees mid-session every lean angle reading from that point forward is off by two degrees. This is not a hypothetical. It is what happens with mounts that rely on friction and spring tension rather than mechanical locking.

THE SP CONNECT SYSTEM

SP Connect is a German company that builds phone mount systems for motorcycles, bicycles, and outdoor sports. Their motorcycle lineup is designed from the ground up for performance riding, not commuting or casual touring. The system is modular, meaning the phone case, the mount head, and the bike attachment are all separate components that work together through a universal twist-lock interface.

The twist-lock mechanism is the defining feature of the SP Connect system. The phone case or phone holder attaches to any SP Connect mount with a quarter-turn twist that locks mechanically. There is no spring, no friction, no clamping force. The lock is positive and audible. You either hear the click and it is locked, or it is not clicked and it is not mounted. There is no ambiguous middle ground where the mount feels secure but is not.

The Moto Stem Mount

The stem mount attaches to the stem bolt on the top triple clamp, the most rigid point on the front end of a sportbike. This position puts the phone directly in the rider's sightline, in the center of the bike's rotation axis, and completely away from the handlebars. Because the stem mount is attached to the frame rather than the bars, handlebar movement during steering does not affect the mount position. The phone stays fixed while the bars move underneath it.

This is a significant advantage over handlebar mounts for telemetry purposes. A handlebar mount moves with the bars through every steering input. A stem mount stays fixed relative to the bike's frame and lean axis. For lean angle measurement, a sensor mounted closer to the bike's rotation axis produces more representative readings of what the bike is actually doing.

The Anti Vibration Module

This is the component that makes the SP Connect setup exceptional for telemetry rather than just good. The Anti Vibration Module is a rubber-isolated damper that sits between the stem mount and the phone holder. It absorbs high-frequency vibration from the engine and road surface before it reaches the phone while maintaining rigid lateral and vertical positioning.

The practical result is that the phone's IMU sees a significantly cleaner signal than it would through a rigid metal mount. Engine vibration that would register as noise in the gyroscope and accelerometer readings is absorbed at the mount before it reaches the sensor. ThrottleX's Kalman filter has less work to do and produces cleaner output. The smoothness score in your ride reports, the lean angle trace, and the G-force readings are all more accurate with the Anti Vibration Module installed than without it.

COMPATIBILITY

SP Connect makes phone cases for virtually every current flagship Android device including Samsung Galaxy S and A series, Google Pixel, and OnePlus. If your specific phone does not have a dedicated SP Connect case available, the Universal Phone Clamp works with any phone up to a certain width and connects to the same twist-lock interface as the dedicated cases.

For most riders the Universal Phone Clamp is actually the better choice for a telemetry setup because you do not need to change cases when you upgrade your phone. The clamp stays on the mount, you swap the phone out of the clamp, and your calibration and mount position are unchanged.

THE CALIBRATION ADVANTAGE

ThrottleX includes a lean angle offset calibration that zeros out the reading when the bike is upright. This calibration accounts for any fixed angle introduced by how the phone sits in the mount. With a rigid, non-moving mount like SP Connect you run this calibration once and it stays valid for the life of the mount installation.

With a flexible or friction-based mount that allows subtle repositioning between sessions, you need to recalibrate every time because you cannot guarantee the phone is at the same angle it was last time. SP Connect eliminates this variable entirely. Calibrate once, ride forever, and trust the numbers every time.
