// Camera Integration

RIDE. RECORD. SYNC.

DJI Osmo Action In Development
Action 4, 5 Pro, and 6 — Bluetooth sync

ThrottleX connects to your DJI Osmo Action camera over Bluetooth and automatically starts and stops recording when you begin and end a session. No tapping two devices. No sync headaches. Both start together and both stop together.

  • Automatic recording trigger when session starts
  • Frame-perfect sync using the .tlx export timestamp
  • Compatible with Osmo Action 4, 5 Pro, and 6
  • Telemetry overlay alignment for video export
GoPro In Development
Hero 9, 10, 11, 12, and 13 — Open GoPro API

The same seamless recording sync built for DJI, now for GoPro riders. Using the Open GoPro API, ThrottleX connects to your Hero camera over Bluetooth and handles everything automatically when you start a session.

  • Automatic recording trigger and stop
  • Open GoPro API integration via Bluetooth
  • Compatible with Hero 9, 10, 11, 12, and 13
  • Same .tlx sync timestamp for overlay alignment

// Video Export

YOUR DATA. ON SCREEN.

Telemetry Overlay Studio Coming Soon
Desktop tool — Windows and Mac

A desktop companion app that takes your camera footage and your ThrottleX .tlx export and produces a finished video with your telemetry data burned in as a live overlay. Lean angle, speed, G-force, and traction efficiency displayed frame-perfect alongside your ride footage.

  • Auto-sync using the .tlx recordStartEpochMs timestamp
  • Lean angle, speed, G-force, and traction efficiency overlays
  • Export to MP4 via ffmpeg
  • Works with DJI and GoPro footage
  • Windows and Mac support

// ECU Integration

STRAIGHT FROM THE ENGINE.

OBD2 Integration Planned
Bluetooth OBD2 adapter — ECU data direct to ThrottleX

The OBD2 integration connects ThrottleX directly to your bike's ECU via a Bluetooth OBD2 adapter. Engine data that phone sensors alone cannot provide — RPM, throttle position, engine temperature, and more depending on what your specific bike exposes over its diagnostic port. The code is written. Real-world testing is next.

  • Bluetooth OBD2 adapter connection
  • Live ECU data displayed alongside telemetry
  • Data logged per session alongside existing sensor data
  • What data is available depends on your bike's ECU
