Crash Detection and SOS

Ride alone. Never be alone.

ThrottleX monitors your IMU in real time using sensor fusion crash detection. A high-G impact above 4.0G or a tip-over beyond 65 degrees triggers a 30 second full-screen countdown. If you do not cancel it, the app sends an SMS to your emergency contact with your GPS coordinates and a Google Maps link. Always-on background mode keeps monitoring even when the app is closed.

  • 4.0G impact detection with sample-window confirmation
  • 65 degree tip-over detection with persistence check
  • 30 second countdown with large cancel button
  • Automated SMS with GPS location to emergency contact
  • Always-on background protection mode
  • Arms automatically at 8 mph or faster
Sensors and Analytics

Professional tools. Phone-based precision.

ThrottleX uses a custom Kalman filter to remove engine vibration noise from the sensor stream. Then it gives you four dedicated tools to understand and calibrate exactly what you are measuring.

Sensor Calibration

Sit upright on the bike and hold to calibrate. ThrottleX zeros the lean angle sensor to your specific phone mount position. Auto-calibrate detects when the bike is stopped and ready.

Lean Distribution Histogram

A real-time statistical breakdown of your cornering habits. Tracks the percentage of ride time spent at each lean angle bracket. Identifies which side you favor and helps you aim for symmetry.

Minimal Ghost Layout

Apex Hunter Layout

Garage and Service Tracker

Your bike. Always maintained.

ThrottleX is a full garage companion. Every service interval is tied to your GPS-tracked odometer so the numbers are always accurate. An animated wrench icon appears on your main dashboard the moment any item is overdue. Never miss a service interval again.

  • GPS-tracked master odometer with background service
  • Trip Meter 1 and Trip Meter 2 both resettable

Bike and Tire Profile

Calibrated for your machine.

ThrottleX is not a one-size-fits-all data logger. The Bike Profile tells the app what kind of machine it is on. Sport, Cruiser, or Touring. It adjusts the sensor fusion physics accordingly. Select your actual tire from the library and the traction model auto-calibrates to your rubber's specific grip profile.

  • Bike Geometry: Sport, Cruiser, or Touring sensor fusion
  • Traction Model: Conservative, Standard, or Performance grip window
  • Sensor Smoothing: Smooth, Neutral, or Quick response
  • Searchable real tire database with profile sharpness values
  • Tire selection auto-calibrates the Friction Circle rendering
  • User-calibrated lean limit for your personal edge angle
