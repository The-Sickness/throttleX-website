Live Telemetry

Real data. Every corner.

ThrottleX pulls from your phone's IMU and GPS simultaneously — no external hardware needed. Lean angle, lateral and longitudinal G-force, and GPS speed all live on one dashboard.

  • Real-time lean angle with traction indicator
  • Lateral & longitudinal G-force + peak capture
  • GPS speed in mph or km/h
  • Top speed tracker per session
Ride History & Analysis

Know your numbers after the ride.

Every session is logged automatically. Pull up your ride history and get a full corner performance breakdown — left vs right symmetry, confidence score, traction efficiency, and performance split by corner type. When you're done reviewing, export your session in any format you need.

  • Corner Confidence Score (0–100%)
  • Left/right lean symmetry analysis
  • Traction efficiency per side
  • Performance split: slow / med / fast corners
Session Export
PDF A full professional ride report — lean angle distribution, G-force log, G-G traction diagram, technical skill radar, left/right symmetry analysis, trail braking scores, and more. Built to share with a coach or just study on your own.
KML Opens in Google Earth or Google Maps. On a track it shows your route, laps, lap times, and color-codes every corner by lean angle. On a point-to-point ride it maps your route with color-coded lean angle on any corner 20°+. Three colors: low, mid, and high lean.
GPX Universal GPS format. Import into Garmin, Strava, Komoot, or any mapping tool that accepts GPS data. Take your ride data wherever you need it.
Export all three formats at once — one tap.
Voice Coach

Your auditory knee puck.

Set a max lean threshold and ThrottleX calls it out through your headset the instant you hit it — no screen glances mid-corner. Choose your warning phrase and set periodic ride stat updates at any interval you want.

  • Headset audio alerts at max lean threshold
  • Custom warning phrases
  • Periodic ride stat callouts
  • Configurable intervals (1 min – 1 hr)

  • GPS-tracked master odometer
  • Oil life monitor with custom interval
  • Chain, brakes, cooling, forks tracking
  • Tire library (Pirelli, Dunlop, and more)

ThrottleX requires Android 8.0 or higher. Android 12+ recommended for best sensor performance. Most phones from 2018 onward work great.
No. ThrottleX uses your phone's built-in IMU and GPS. No OBD dongle, no Bluetooth sensors, no external anything. Just your phone, a mount, and your bike.
With a solid handlebar mount and a modern phone, ThrottleX delivers lean angle accuracy within ±0.5° of actual. Use the Lean Angle Offset calibration to zero it out perfectly for your specific mount position.
Not at all. ThrottleX works anywhere you ride — canyon runs, backroads, commutes, or the track. The four ride modes (Road, Track, Rain, Night) only change the color of the UI. They don't affect sensors or data in any way. Sensors run at full precision in every mode, all the time.
ThrottleX works with any motorcycle. The Bike Profile setting (Sport / Cruiser / Touring) lets you tell the app your geometry so sensor fusion is tuned for your specific bike's physics.
